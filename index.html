ABOUT THE FILM

Music that speaks when words cannot.

Silent Resonance follows a young man with autism whose talent as a pianist becomes a powerful form of self-expression. With the support of those around him, he is given an opportunity to step forward, be seen, and show what he is capable of.

“The film is also personal to me and was created in part to bring more awareness and conversation around autism within our communities.”
— Cesar Cage, Writer & Director

ABOUT THE FILMMAKER

Cesar Cage

Writer • Director • Producer

Cesar Cage is a Bronx-based filmmaker, actor, writer, director and producer, and the founder of Regen – One Visions. Silent Resonance is his first completed short film as writer/director, created with the goal of telling human stories that bring people together and remind us that despite our differences, we’re not all that different.

HAVEN

The next film from Regen – One Visions

Three years after a devastating outbreak has torn civilization apart, James is still haunted by the disappearance of the woman he loved, who was taken by a mysterious group known as the Disciples. While trying to survive alongside a small group of survivors, James discovers that she may not only still be alive, but has become a central figure in a much larger conflict surrounding Haven—a place that may represent humanity’s best chance at survival.
